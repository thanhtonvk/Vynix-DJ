Vynix DJ is a digital DJ application based on Mixxx, built for touch screens
and USB DJ controllers. Wave, Ogg, FLAC and MP3 files can be mixed for live
performances with sync, loops, Beat FX and Rekordbox USB drives.

Vynix DJ works with ALSA or Jack, can be controlled from the GUI or from
external controllers, including MIDI devices, and supports skins.
